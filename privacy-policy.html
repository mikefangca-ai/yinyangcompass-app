Privacy Policy — YinYang Compass

Last updated: June 11, 2026

The short version: YinYang Compass collects no data. Nothing you enter ever leaves your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

Information We Collect

None. YinYang Compass does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information Stored on Your Device

To function, the app keeps the following only on your device, in the app's local storage:

Your name (or any name you choose to enter), your birth date and time, and the reading history you create. This information is used solely to calculate and display your yin-yang profile and readings within the app. It is never transmitted anywhere, and we have no ability to access it.

Deleting Your Data

Open the app's Profile tab and tap "Start Over" to permanently erase all stored information. Deleting the app from your device also removes all data.

Third-Party Services

The app uses no third-party SDKs, no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and makes no network connections.

Children's Privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If a future version of the app ever changes these practices, this policy will be updated and the change will be clearly described before any new data practice takes effect.
